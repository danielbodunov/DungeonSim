# Development Log - 2026-08-09

This entry records the design and implementation work completed on August 9, 2026.

## Dungeon building and generation

- **Added:** Persistent `Auto`, `Narrow`, and `Wide` cell-width intentions with matching build-palette controls.
- **Added:** A `Toggle Wall` interaction for clicking the shared boundary between two built cells.
- **Updated:** Local tile resolution so editing a cell or wall re-evaluates the affected region without rebuilding the entire dungeon.
- **Improved:** Wide-room generation so sufficiently open neighboring cells can resolve into wide tiles naturally.
- **Improved:** Narrow corridor generation by allowing adjacent corridors to remain separated by an explicit wall.
- **Fixed:** Right-click block removal so failed removal no longer leaves an incorrectly darkened section of the dungeon.
- **Removed:** The separate Connected and Corridor build modes after shared-wall editing made them redundant.

## Ladder generation and platforms

- **Added:** Ladder lane IDs and compatible lane IDs, including support for left and right continuation lanes.
- **Added:** Traversal-exit and platform policies that distinguish automatically generated platforms from future manually placed platforms.
- **Updated:** Ladder regeneration so unchanged runs are preserved while runs touching changed cells are re-evaluated.
- **Improved:** Ladder run validation using authored sockets, lane compatibility, usable walkable exits, and affected-cell fingerprints.
- **Fixed:** Floating ladders, missing ladders, incorrect starter platforms, and intermediate platforms generated without a usable adjacent ledge.
- **Fixed:** Automatic intermediate platforms appearing in wide floor openings; their platform-capable sockets remain available for future manual placement.

## NPC navigation and gameplay

- **Added:** Trap dodge resolution using Dexterity as the primary attribute and Luck as a secondary influence.
- **Added:** Brief rising and fading action feedback for dodges, damage, and defeats.
- **Added:** Recovery from unintended falls, including a configurable recovery delay and fall damage.
- **Added:** Dread rewards for exploration, damage, and level-scaled defeats, with save support for Dread and dungeon level.
- **Improved:** Ground probing around narrow collider seams to reduce accidental falls and navigation stalls.
- **Improved:** Ladder traversal depth so NPCs follow the ladder's Z position with a configurable offset.
- **Improved:** Post-ladder routing so NPCs can continue directly toward their next waypoint.
- **Fixed:** NPCs becoming permanently immobile after slipping between a platform and its tile.
- **Removed:** The forced destination-cell-center waypoint after ladder traversal.

## Camera and interface

- **Updated:** Perspective camera zoom from changing field of view to moving the camera toward or away from its focus plane.
- **Improved:** Camera panning and zoom smoothing so dolly movement keeps the dungeon focus stable.
- **Added:** Visual and interaction design planning for build-mode lighting, selection details, camera focus, NPC following, the build budget, dungeon XP, Dread, unlock milestones, and technology trees.
- **Added:** Main-menu planning for New Game, Load Game, Continue, and Options.
- **Added:** A shared UI-theme decision so interface styling can be controlled consistently from one source.

## Documentation

- **Updated:** Project documentation into `Design`, `TODO`, `Decisions`, `features`, and dated `Logs` sections.
- **Added:** Decision records for phase-based lighting, selection and camera focus, progression interfaces, main-menu entry, and shared UI theming.
- **Improved:** World-generation, building, NPC-behavior, and visual-interaction plans with implementation guidance and acceptance criteria.

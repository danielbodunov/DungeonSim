# 0005: Dread and NPC Defeat

- Status: Amended by t016
- Date: 2026-08-09

## Context

Dungeon growth needs a reward generated by adventurer experiences, and NPC defeat must not permanently exhaust the population of experienced adventurers.

## Decision

Dread is the final name for the dungeon's supernatural progression resource. It is not the ordinary construction currency. Visit rewards accumulate in a pending ledger and settle when an NPC exits, is defeated, or is forcibly returned. Defeat ends the visit but does not permanently kill the character.

## Consequences

- Supernatural growth purchases, beginning with treasure manifestation, compete for banked Dread.
- Mundane construction uses separate physical resources as those systems are introduced.
- Settlement needs a stable visit ID and idempotent payout.
- Recovery time and other consequences of defeat remain open balancing choices.

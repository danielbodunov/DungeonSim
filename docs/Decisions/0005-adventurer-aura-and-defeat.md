# 0005: Adventurer Aura and NPC Defeat

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-09

## Context

Dungeon growth needs a reward generated by adventurer experiences, and NPC defeat must not permanently exhaust the population of experienced adventurers.

## Decision

Adventurer Aura is the working name for one spendable resource used for both construction and irreversible dungeon level upgrades. Visit rewards accumulate in a pending ledger and settle when an NPC exits, is defeated, or is forcibly returned. Defeat ends the visit but does not permanently kill the character.

## Consequences

- Building competes directly with saving for the next dungeon level.
- Purchased levels never regress when later Aura is spent.
- Settlement needs a stable visit ID and idempotent payout.
- Recovery time and other consequences of defeat remain open balancing choices.

See [Dungeon Progression and Adventurer Aura](../Design/World_Generation_and_Building.md#dungeon-progression-and-adventurer-aura) and [NPC Visit Completion](../Design/NPC_Behavior.md#visit-completion-defeat-and-aura-settlement).

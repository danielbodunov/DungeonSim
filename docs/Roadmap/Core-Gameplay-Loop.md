# Core Gameplay Loop Roadmap

## Purpose

DungeonSim is a dungeon-management simulation in which the player acts as a sinister force cultivating a dungeon to attract and manipulate adventurers.

Core loop:

**Build & bait → Adventurers enter → Explore / suffer / steal → Escape or die → Harvest Aura / recover spoils / lose bait → Improve dungeon → Attract more valuable prey**

See `docs/Design/Core_Game_Direction.md` for the standing game-direction principles.

Treasure is dungeon-owned bait, not an automatic player reward. Death is valuable but should not necessarily become the only desirable expedition outcome. Future survivor/reputation systems may make escapes useful even when they carry treasure away.

Tower-defense-style raids remain a possible future bonus mode and should not drive current core architecture.

Ticket IDs are stable and are not renumbered when the roadmap changes.

## Slice A — Sinister Dungeon Expedition

**Milestone outcome:** Treasure functions as bait with real ownership consequences; death recovers spoils and harvests Aura; escape can carry dungeon value away; each expedition has an explicit outcome.

### t005 — NPC Treasure Discovery & Investigation
**Status:** Complete

NPC discovers treasure only after physically reaching its cell, investigates through the existing POI flow, and resolves it exactly once.

Depends on: t004

### t006 — Treasure Pickup & Ownership
**Status:** Complete

Successful treasure investigation transfers dungeon-owned treasure into adventurer-carried loot. No player reward is generated by pickup.

Depends on: t005

### t007 — Adventurer Loot Drop & Dungeon Recovery
**Status:** Planned

Adventurer death returns carried dungeon treasure to a recoverable dungeon-side state and establishes a future-compatible path for spoils the adventurer brought with them.

Depends on: t006

### t008 — Successful Escape & Lost Treasure
**Status:** Planned

An adventurer who escapes with dungeon treasure genuinely removes that value from dungeon ownership. Escape is recorded without yet treating it as reputation gain or generic player failure.

Depends on: t007

### t009 — Soul / Aura Harvesting Foundation
**Status:** Planned

Adventurer death produces a configurable Aura harvest through the existing economy. Architecture remains open to later non-death harvest sources such as fear, injury, traps, exploration, or magic use.

Depends on: t005

### t010 — Expedition Outcomes
**Status:** Planned

Formalize one authoritative visit result such as successful escape/return or defeated/killed, with enough context for loot, Aura, and future story/reputation systems.

Depends on: t007, t008, t009

### t011 — Sinister Dungeon Vertical Slice Validation
**Status:** Planned

Validate the complete bait → explore → steal → escape/die → harvest/recover loop and assess whether the player's manipulation of adventurers creates understandable consequences and small emergent stories.

Depends on: t002–t010

## Slice B — Meaningful Exploration

**Milestone outcome:** NPCs make varied, understandable exploration choices using only personal knowledge, and treasure/other lures can influence those choices once known.

Ticket IDs for this slice will be assigned after t011 validation. Planned subjects include:

- frontier classification (unexplored / partial / familiar transit / exhausted);
- weighted junction selection;
- known-treasure attraction and greed pressure;
- exploration vertical-slice validation.

The old t008–t011 exploration numbering was intentionally superseded before implementation; stable IDs t008–t011 now belong to the Sinister Dungeon Expedition slice and must not be reused again.

---

## Slice C — Dungeon Economy & Growth

**Milestone outcome:** Aura and recovered material value give the player meaningful choices for dungeon expansion, bait investment, traps, and progression.

Planned subjects, to be ticketed after earlier validation:

- atomic build costs;
- dungeon-level/progression spending;
- treasure acquisition/reinvestment and bait economy;
- economy vertical-slice validation.

Do not assume stolen treasure should convert directly into Aura. Soul energy and material loot serve different thematic functions unless later design proves a single-currency prototype is preferable.

---

## Slice D — Risk, Greed & Story Pressure

Potential later systems:

- return-stamina estimation;
- carried-loot influence on greed/retreat decisions;
- risk-scaled bait/value;
- fear and other Aura-harvest sources;
- adventurer personality differences;
- survivor reputation/notoriety;
- stronger/wealthier adventurer attraction;
- observable expedition histories and story-facing summaries.

These should be introduced only after the basic sinister-dungeon loop is fun and legible.

---

## Deferred / Future Modes

Defer until the management loop is proven:

- party formation and detailed social behavior;
- full equipment/inventory systems;
- loot rarity/itemization;
- full combat expansion;
- sophisticated personality architecture;
- large visual-polish passes;
- elaborate UI;
- advanced trap-disarming systems;
- tower-defense-style organized raids.

### Tower-Defense-Style Raids

Organized raids may later exist as a bonus/alternate mode using the same dungeon, traps, monsters, and NPC systems. They are not part of the core progression loop and should not impose requirements on current architecture.

## Gameplay Loop Review Questions

At validation milestones ask:

- Does the player feel like they are manipulating adventurers rather than merely watching pathfinding?
- Is treasure clearly valuable bait that can actually be lost?
- Are death, escape, and retreat meaningfully different outcomes?
- Does death provide satisfying supernatural/material value without making instant-kill design obviously optimal?
- Can the player understand why an adventurer made important choices?
- Do expeditions create memorable small stories?
- Does dungeon layout meaningfully influence those stories?
- Does the resulting Aura/loot create an interesting next management decision?
